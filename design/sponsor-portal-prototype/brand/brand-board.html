What this means for our gala app

Match (identity)

  • Orange #C64C28 for primary buttons, links, accents. Hover #BA4926.
  • Poppins for headings and UI; Noto Sans TC / KR for Chinese and Korean.
  • Bold, short headlines in black; orange accent bar or rule as the signature detail.
  • Pill buttons (50px radius), 600 weight.
  • Black footer with the white logo; the orange mark as app icon.
  • Traditional Chinese, as on aafe.org.

Ours to adapt (product, not website)

  • Our cards may keep soft radius and glass chrome: the site has no app UI to copy.
  • Body 16–18px instead of 20px: denser screens, iPad check-in.
  • Sentence-case buttons inside the app; UPPERCASE only for one hero CTA.
  • Status colors (green / amber / grey / red) are ours; AAFE has none.
  • Replace Fraunces serif titles and #E0532A orange (white text only 3.86:1).
