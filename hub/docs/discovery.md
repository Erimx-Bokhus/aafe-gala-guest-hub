# Discovery

What AAFE told us on the October 1 call, in their words where it matters. AAFE's gala lead ran the guest list for the 2026 gala while AAFE's project contact was on leave.

## The top priority

> "A way to layer on top of GiveLively to collect that information in a more seamless way, both for staff as well as for the sponsor." — AAFE's project contact

- **Sponsorship guest names are the biggest pain.** Individual tickets are "much more manageable."
- **Sponsors should fix their own lists.** AAFE's gala lead wants guests and sponsors to "go back and update if needed."
- **Every record should trace to its source.** AAFE's project contact described "the marriage of different sources … and being able to link back to whatever data source it came from."

## How it works today

- **Individual tickets** are bought on Give Lively, downloaded, and pasted into the spreadsheet.
- **Sponsors** often pledge by email or verbally, then pay by check or ACH to avoid Give Lively's fees. Staff email them for guest names and logos by a deadline.
- **Package sizes:** the top tier holds about 20 tickets and a middle tier about 10. A $25,000 gift comes with 7 tickets; $1,000 comes with 2.
- **Comps** go to community partners and elected officials, tracked by whichever staff member holds the relationship.
- **The spreadsheet also tracks** logo collection and payment type and date, which AAFE's fiscal team uses to tag gala revenue.
- **Name tags** are a mail merge. In 2026, wrong titles and affiliations were caught only because a relationship manager glanced at them just before printing.
- **Registration links out.** aafe.org/gala sends buyers to Give Lively; the form is not embedded.
- **Tried before:** an expensive ticketing platform AAFE couldn't afford, and an auto-sent Google Form with low response that never reached verbal pledges.

## Gala night

- Check-in runs from a printed spreadsheet, and the line is slow.
- Sponsor guests who were never named get pre-printed blank name tags with the company name.
- Walk-ins happen. A check-in lead decides, and AAFE plans to seat relationship managers at the table.
- AAFE wants iPad check-in and a record of who actually attended.
- AAFE's gala lead liked emailed QR-code tickets scanned at the door, and comp tickets relationship managers can send themselves.

## Other requirements

- **Seating** is lower priority. Staff assign seats, partly because some funders shouldn't sit together. The table number on the badge matters most.
- **Emails** need staff approval before sending.
- **Salesforce** goes live for the 2027 gala. An export into it "would be great."
- **Design** should match aafe.org, which AAFE's communications team designed.

## Constraints

> "Tech capacity on our team is pretty low … we don't have capacity to build something new or maintain something that's very custom." — AAFE's gala lead

- AAFE uses Google Workspace and Google Drive.
- AAFE's project contact is finalizing an AI acceptable-use policy (no personal information into AI tools) and wants to be kept close on data decisions.
