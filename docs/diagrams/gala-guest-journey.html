How a guest gets from purchase to the door

Three ways onto the guest list: a Give Lively purchase, a check or pledge, or a comp. All three land on one guest list, which feeds gala night and the Salesforce file. Orange steps are where AAFE staff approve before anything goes out.

Comp guest

Invited by the Executive Director, board or a relationship manager.

  1. Staff add the guest

    Name, title, organization, email, and a note on who invited them. They are on the guest list from this moment.

    AAFE staff Relationship guests

  2. Staff send the QR invitation

    One button: "Send QR invite". The staff member who sends it is the approver, and the send is logged.

    Staff approve

  3. The tool makes a personal QR code

    Created at the moment the email sends, one per guest, attached as an image. Only a scrambled copy is kept, so a leaked list can't be used to get in.

    Automatic

  4. Seat and badge

    Same as every other guest.

    AAFE staff

One guest list

Every guest from all three lanes, with where each record came from and every change logged. Staff corrections survive the next Give Lively upload.

  • Named guests go on the iPad list as soon as they're named, paid or not
  • Unnamed sponsor seats show at the door, for walk-ins on that sponsor's table
  • A full refund or a failed payment cancels the order and its seats; a partial refund keeps them, as partly paid

Gala night: iPad check-in

  • Scan the QR code on the guest's phone or printout, or search the name. Every guest can be found by name; a QR code is a shortcut. One tap on Check in checks a guest in, with Undo for 10 seconds.
  • Walk-ins and exceptions are added at the door with a reason, on the sponsor's order if they have one.
  • Works offline. Check-ins wait on the iPad and send when the internet is back. Signing out never deletes ones that haven't sent.

After the gala

  • Who came (still to build): arrivals and no-shows per sponsor.
  • Salesforce: a file shaped for AAFE's Salesforce import, or Sync now to send it (a stand-in Salesforce in the demo). Nothing writes to Salesforce on its own. Give Lively's own app already sends its donations there every hour; ours adds who came and who each sponsor brought.
  • Clean-up (AAFE to decide): contact, dietary and access details deleted on AAFE's schedule.

How AAFE staff handle comps and QR codes

How does a comp get into the app?
Staff add them on the Relationship guests page: name, title, organization, email and who invited them. That puts them on the guest list and on the iPad list at the same time.
Who generates the QR codes?
Nobody does it by hand. When staff press Send QR invite, the tool creates a new personal code, attaches it to the email, and sends it. Pressing it again sends a fresh code and the old one stops working.
What if a comp never gets the email or forgets the code?
The door finds them by name. The QR code only makes check-in faster.
The QR codes in the demo?
Those are invented demo codes so the iPad can be tried without sending email. In the real tool every code is made at send time.
