Where the information goes

The guest-list database is in the middle. Purchases come in from Give Lively, AAFE staff approve what goes out, sponsors name their guests, and the iPad takes the door list to the gala. Each kind of line moves the way the information moves. Pick a story to follow one path.

How a QR code is made

  1. Staff add the comp on Relationship guests in the staff app. The guest is on the door list from now on.
  2. Staff press Send QR invite. The person who presses it is the approver, and it's logged.
  3. The email sender makes a brand-new random code, attaches it to the email as a QR image, and sends it.
  4. The database keeps only a scrambled copy. The iPad gets that copy with the door list and checks scanned codes against it, even offline.
  5. Sending again makes a new code, and the old one stops working. Codes expire the morning after the gala.
