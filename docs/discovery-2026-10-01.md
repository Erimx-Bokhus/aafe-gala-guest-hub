## How it works today
- Individual tickets: bought on Give Lively, downloaded, pasted into the spreadsheet.
- Sponsors: many skip Give Lively to avoid fees; they pledge by email or verbally, then pay by check or ACH. Staff email them for guest names and logos by a deadline, then type the names into the spreadsheet.
- Package sizes: top tier ~20 tickets, middle tier ~10; e.g., $25,000 → 7 tickets, $1,000 → 2 tickets. Benefits also include logo on signage and the digital program.
- Comps: given to community partners, tracked by individual staff based on relationships.
- Spreadsheet also tracks logo collection and **payment type and date** (fiscal team uses it to tag gala revenue).
- Name tags: mail merge from the spreadsheet. 2026: wrong titles and affiliations caught only because a relationship manager glanced at them right before printing.
- Registration is **not embedded** on aafe.org; aafe.org/gala links out to Give Lively.
- Tried before: an expensive ticketing platform (unaffordable), and an auto-sent Google Form (low response; never reached verbal pledges).
