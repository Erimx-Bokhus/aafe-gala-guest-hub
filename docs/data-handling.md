## Commitments
1. No payment processing: no Stripe integration; no card, bank, or ACH details. Payments are recorded as method, date, status, and amount.
2. No real donor data during the capstone; synthetic data through Demo Day (Nov 10).
3. No AI in the first version (decided Oct 17, `decisions.md`); the tool has no AI code. If AAFE wants AI later, it is optional and supervised: minimum fields, no emails/phones/payment info sent to AI, staff approve everything, one admin switch turns AI off. Use a paid provider whose terms exclude training on submitted data; confirm terms in writing before go-live.
4. AAFE decides retention. Proposed default: Salesforce-ready attendee export after the gala, then delete guest contact details within 60 days (our own backup copies too: Supabase's free plan keeps none), dietary and accessibility details included. Dietary and accessibility answers are **not** kept from year to year (decided Oct 18, `decisions.md`): each guest's answers are deleted after the gala, and AAFE keeps only that gala's totals (how many guests chose each option, for example 42 vegan, 3 wheelchair access), with no names attached. Until then only AAFE staff and the sponsor contact who entered them (through their private link) see them, and they are never sent to Salesforce or to AI tools. **Built (Oct 18):** after the gala, a gala admin saves the food and access totals and deletes every guest's answers (#194); if no one does, the 60-day job below deletes them, so they're gone 60 days after the gala at the latest. Guests' contact details are deleted 60 days after the gala by a daily job, or earlier by a gala admin, with every copy: the change log's old values, the email log's addresses, review items, Give Lively's guest lists and raw records; names and sponsors' own details stay. The 60 days are the proposed default, built ahead of AAFE's answer (`decisions.md`): a different answer changes one number. The staff app's buttons are still to come.

## Stores
Purchaser/sponsor contact; attendee name, title, organization, email (optional or required: still open in `decisions.md`; the sponsor portal and the relationship-guest form require it today, and the server accepts a guest without one); package and seats; payment method/date/status label, and for checks the check number; relationship owner, and for a comp guest staff's own note on the relationship (`relationship_note`: free text, seen only in the staff app and the Full guest list report, kept after the gala; the 60-day deletion doesn't clear it, so it shouldn't hold anyone's contact details); logo/program status; table number, badge status, check-in time; source of each record; change history (the app shows its summaries; old values stay in the database for the record, except food and access answers, which are cleared with the answers after the gala). Give Lively's own records of this gala's purchases, without phone numbers or postal addresses. Optional (AAFE's choice): dietary/accessibility needs, deleted after the gala with only that gala's totals kept (decided Oct 18, `decisions.md`); door lists show only a yes/no accessibility flag. The check-in iPad keeps the door list (names, titles, organizations, tables, the yes/no flag) until someone signs out or the gala closes.

## Never stores
Card numbers, bank/ACH details, check images, Stripe or Give Lively credentials, sponsor passwords.

## Access
Staff sign in with a one-time code or link emailed to them: people on the staff list, and, until the end of Nov 21, 2026, anyone with an @aafe.org or @pursuit.org email, who joins as a relationship manager on their first sign-in (decided Oct 21, for testing with invented data; it ends on its own on that date). Roles: gala admin / relationship manager / check-in volunteer. Relationship managers see guests' emails and food and access details for the gala being run only; gala admins see every year, and Give Lively's raw records. Sponsor links and QR e-tickets are random, stored only as a fingerprint (hash), scoped to one purchase or one seat, and expire (links at AAFE's deadline, QR codes after gala night); a change log of every edit, which gala admins read (relationship managers see only what sponsors changed in their portal and what Give Lively recorded, decided Oct 22); encryption in transit and at rest; secrets in the host's secrets store. Services that receive data: Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (the apps, and Cloudflare Access, which keeps the email address of everyone who signs in to open them), Google (the team Gmail account that sends sign-in codes: the recipient's address and the code), Resend (emails: the recipient's name and address and the email's text, which carries the private link or QR code), and, if AAFE connects it, Salesforce (a gala admin sends sponsors' organization names, pledge amounts and stages, their check, ACH and wire payments with amount, date and method, and guests' names, emails and attendance status; never phones, food or access details; the tool's sync log keeps Salesforce's reason for each record it refused, with any email in it hidden, #252). Downloaded reports are copies the tool can't delete: files with guests' emails or food and access details (Who came, Full guest list: gala admins only) belong in AAFE's own drive, not on a shared iPad, and are deleted under the same retention rule.

## Open decisions for AAFE
Retention window for contact details (60 days built as the proposed default); who the gala admins are; production host and technical partner (after Demo Day); AI only if AAFE wants it later, under AAFE's acceptable-use policy (commitment 3).
