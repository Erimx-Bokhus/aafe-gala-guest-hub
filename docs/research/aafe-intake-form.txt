02. Project Overview
Project Title: Gala Guest List Management

Short Description (1-3 sentences): AAFE’s largest fundraising campaign each year is our annual gala,
hosting 500-600 guests including sponsors, partners, funders, and community members. The guest list is
currently managed by staff in a bulky and manual spreadsheet. We would like a tool to collect and
consolidate guest names & other information, accept changes, and identify gaps in our list.

What problem is this project addressing, and why does it matter to your organization (1-2 sentences)?
This project would allow us to have a tool without paying an expensive subscription fee for a fundraising
event platform, while also saving significant staff time currently allocated to guest list management during a
crucial period of event preparation. Using a manual spreadsheet also creates room for error, but these are
some of our most important relationships; ensuring a smooth guest experience is a high priority for us.

Output of the Project:
We need a tool that will give us a complete, correct, and up-to-date guest list before our gala takes place
so we can print name tags and check people in at the event. Ideally, this tool would work with our existing
fundraising platform, GiveLively.

Guided Questions: Core Business Questions Dashboard should Help Answer for Mock Investor
   ●​ Who is planning on attending the gala, and have we received all relevant information, payment,
       and materials from them?
   ●​ For each guest, who on AAFE staff is responsible for owning that relationship?
   ●​ How are we tracking to goal on sponsorships, tickets, and other categories of attendees?
03. Project Scope
  ●​ Suggested Deliverables:
        ○​ Tool that connects to GiveLively or other inputs to create a guest list that includes name,
            email, company, ticket tier, table number, AAFE relationship owner, # of tickets in party,
            payment status, guest list status, and marketing materials status
        ○​ Potentially this tool will also be able to prompt e-mail communications for sponsors or
            guests that we are missing information from
        ○​ Ideally, this tool could be used for live check-in at the event to track which guests actually
            attend vs. only reserve tickets​

  ●​ Known Constraints (optional):
        ○​ GiveLively’s current offering under our subscription has a couple of challenges.
           Sponsorship levels and ticket packages come with different #s of guests, so requesting
           guest names & contact information requires conditional questions that GiveLively doesn’t
           offer. Additionally, GiveLively does not support a sponsor making changes once they add a
           guest name.
        ○​ Many of our tickets are purchased by someone who is not attending (e.g., a coordinator
           purchases a ticket for their manager or others in their company); we need to differentiate
           between contact information and attendee information.
        ○​ Guests pay and register via different methods. We try to encourage everyone to go
           through our event website, but we also accept sponsorship dollars via check and direct
           deposit, which requires manual additions to our guest list.
        ○​ We also comp a number of tickets that are not tracked for payment but need to be tracked
           for RSVP purposes, like elected officials.​
